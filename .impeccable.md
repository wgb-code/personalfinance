# Design Context: Personal Finance Organizer

## Users

**Primary**: Couples managing shared household finances together  
**Context**: Using on various devices (desktop for planning, mobile for quick transactions)  
**Emotions**: Calm, intentional, trustworthy  
**Job to be done**: "I want to see exactly where our money goes and plan together"

## Brand Personality

Three words: **Calm. Clear. Honest.**

- Calm: No pressure, no anxiety-inducing red/warnings, spacious layout
- Clear: Every number, date, label is immediately scannable. No hidden complexity.
- Honest: Show reality (overspending, savings, trends) without judgment

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual Tone**: Refined minimalism with purposeful warmth. NOT trendy, NOT generic SaaS purple gradients.

**Reference Points** (what NOT to copy, but direction):
- Personal finance apps that feel like a trusted advisor (not a corporate dashboard)
- Swiss design (clear typography, structured grids, generous whitespace)
- Warm but neutral (soft greens/blues for growth, warm grays for stability — NOT neon)

**Theme**: Light mode primary (couples planning at kitchen table, daytime context). Dark mode available for evening review.

**Typography**:
- Display font: Something warm and human (not cold geometric sans). Preference: friendly serif or rounded sans.
- Body font: Highly readable, excellent at small sizes (numbers matter). Something like a refined system font.
- Hierarchy: Clear contrast between labels, values, headers. Use weight + size strategically.

## Design Principles

1. **Data First**: Numbers and trends are the content. Everything else serves them.
2. **Accessibility by Default**: Focus states visible, high contrast, semantic HTML. Not an afterthought.
3. **Spacious Not Dense**: Whitespace is a feature. Columns breathe. Cards don't nest inside cards.
4. **Warm Minimalism**: Design is confident enough to say "no" to decorative elements. But not cold.
5. **Couple-Centric**: Views for "me" and "us" should feel natural. Not bolted-on.

## Technical Constraints

- React 19 + Vite (fast, lightweight)
- shadcn/ui (Radix Nova) + Tailwind CSS v4
- Responsive: mobile-first, works on all screen sizes
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Performance: 80+ Lighthouse score on all metrics

## Key Anti-Patterns to Avoid

❌ Bright gradients (AI slop #1)  
❌ Card-heavy layouts with heavy shadows  
❌ Border-left stripes on alerts (AI slop #2)  
❌ Gradient text or decorative flourishes  
❌ Overuse of icons/illustrations  
❌ Defaulting to dark mode "because it looks cool"

## Color Palette Direction

**Light Mode**:
- Background: Warm white (not pure white)
- Foreground text: Deep neutral (almost black, tiny warmth)
- Primary accent: Calm teal or soft green (growth/forward)
- Secondary: Warm gray (stability, paid/historical)
- Alert: Soft orange (caution, not alarming red)

**Dark Mode**:
- Background: Deep neutral
- Foreground: Soft white (high contrast but not harsh)
- Primary: Same teal/green (slightly brighter)
- Secondary: Warmer gray
- Alert: Soft orange (consistent)

## Voice & Microcopy

- "Saving for your trip" (not "Goal: Trip")
- "€150 — due in 5 days" (not "URGENT: R$150 DUE SOON!!")
- "No bills this month" (empty state is normal, not a problem)
- Numbers are always formatted: R$ 1.234,56 (never "1234.56")

---

This design context should guide all UI decisions. When in doubt: ask "Does this help clarity or trust?" If no, remove it.
